# Verified exercises

In “Learn” mode, open the logo menu, then **Exercises**. Pick a level (high school, bachelor, master, olympiad) and a domain. Statements are in French, Malagasy and English.

## Answering

- **In Lean**: write only what follows `by`. The statement is fixed by the exercise; Lean accepts or refuses.
- **In words**: a model translates your argument into Lean, and Lean checks that translation. Read the Lean proof shown: it is what is verified, not your words. A refusal does not mean the statement is false.

## Hints and solution

Hints come one by one, after a first attempt. The solution comes next, only if Lean accepts it; “Explain” reads it line by line. Your progress stays on the host computer.

## For teachers

At the bottom of the list, “Teacher tools”:

- **Make it an exercise** (also in a dossier's ⋯ menu): from a dossier or the library;
- **Worksheet**: tick exercises, then PDF or LaTeX, with or without the verified solutions;
- **Exercise bank**: import or export a file.

## Interface in Malagasy

Settings › Language › Malagasy. The translation is assisted and to be reviewed; what is not translated yet is shown in French.
